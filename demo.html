About

A six-room guesthouse run by the Ceesay family since 2014, two minutes' walk from Tanji's fishing beach. Rooms are simple, clean and fan-cooled, with a shaded courtyard where breakfast is served.

We work directly with the fish market and with two local birdwatching guides, so guests can book a morning on the water without a middleman.
